## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Export CSV: an "Export CSV" button beside Import CSV downloads every holding at current prices; the Overview detail inspection's footer exports its scope (whole portfolio, raw only or sealed only) at the snapshot being viewed; and the asset detail sheet exports that one holding. Files come from `GET /api/export` (`?scope=portfolio|raw|sealed`, or `?raw=<entry id>` / `?sealed=<product id>`, plus `?snapshot=YYYY-MM-DD`), one row per holding with kind, card id, name, set, number, rarity, variant, condition, product type, SKU, quantity, market price, value, low, high, unit cost, cost basis, gain, gain %, change since last snapshot, share of portfolio and the as-of day. Amounts are in the selected currency (named in a `currency` column) and rounded to its decimals. The identifying columns match the card and sealed importers, so a file can be imported again; money columns avoid the importers' purchase-price names because they are not in USD. Files are UTF-8 with a BOM for Excel, and text that would run as a spreadsheet formula is prefixed with `'`. Logic in `src/lib/export.ts` with unit tests (#47).
- Asset detail ("Position telemetry") for single holdings: tap or click a row in Most valuable and Top sealed holdings on the Overview, or in the Top 8 leaderboard and Top movers on Raw Cards, to see that stack's market value, quantity × unit price, share of the portfolio, low and high estimate, cost basis, gain / loss and, for raw cards, the change since the last snapshot, in the selected currency. Below 640px it, and the Overview detail inspection, open as a bottom sheet that slides up and can be swiped down from its grab handle (respecting reduced motion and the phone's safe area); on larger screens they stay a centred modal. Rows are buttons, so they work with the keyboard too. Figures come from `rawAsset` / `sealedAsset` in `src/lib/asset-detail.ts` with unit tests (#46).
- Detail inspection on the Overview: every card has an inspect button (top right, shown on hover or focus on desktop, always on phones) that opens a modal for its scope: the whole portfolio, sealed only or raw only. It shows expanded metrics (midpoint and low–high range, cost basis, gain / loss, holdings, split or units, largest holding), volatility over the last 90 days of snapshots (change, typical move as the std dev between snapshots, best and worst move, max drawdown) and a liquidation projection: net proceeds for a quick, fair and patient sale at the low, mid and high estimate after an illustrative 13 % selling fee, with the fair sale against cost. Escape, a click outside or Done closes it and focus returns to the button. The modal is rendered on the server, so it follows the selected currency and snapshot. Calculations live in `src/lib/inspection.ts` (`volatility`, `liquidation`, `getValueSeries`) with unit tests (#45).
- Reorderable Overview cards: a "Reorder layout" button in the dashboard header turns on reorder mode, where every card shows ◀ ::: ▶ controls. Drag a card onto another to take its place, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons (keyboard and touch friendly; focus stays on the button and screen readers hear the new position). "Reset to default order" in the reorder banner restores the default. The order is saved per browser in localStorage (`overview-card-order`); cards added later appear at their default position. While reordering, the cards' own controls are inactive so a drag never edits a label or follows a link. Order logic lives in `src/lib/card-order.ts` with unit tests (#44).
- Every view now shows money in the selected currency: Overview (summary cards, YTD and 30-day comparisons, value over time, value mix, most valuable, valuation & concentration, top sealed holdings, collection structure), Raw Cards (all panels, the all-cards table, grid and by-set views, totals) and Scenarios (summary, projection chart and outcomes). Chart axes and tooltips included. Chart data is converted before plotting, so axis ticks are round in that currency (e.g. ¥1.5M, ¥3M). The Raw Cards header says which currency values are shown in. A page converts at one rate, the selected currency's rate for the day being shown (a snapshot uses that day's stored rate), so history and comparisons show portfolio moves, not exchange-rate moves (#42).
- Currency selector in the header (in the menu drawer below 1024px): a searchable dropdown of the 10 currencies with flag, code, symbol, name and today's rate per 1 USD, plus the rate date. Search matches code, name, country or symbol, and the arrow keys, Enter and Escape work. The choice is saved in a `currency` cookie for a year, so the server can render every view in it without a flash of USD (`getCurrency()` in `src/lib/currency-pref.ts`). Every view applies it as of #42 (#41).
- Currency formatting helpers in `src/lib/format.ts`: `money`, `wholeMoney`, `compactMoney` and `signedMoney` convert a USD amount into a display currency and format it with that currency's symbol and decimals (JPY has none), e.g. €92.00, ¥190,740, $16.8k, ¥2.6M. Currency symbols, names, flags and decimals from the design are in `CURRENCY_INFO`. The existing `usd`, `wholeUsd`, `compactUsd` and `signed` now wrap these with USD, so the app looks the same until views take the selected currency (#40).
- Exchange rates for multi-currency display: a `fx_rates` table (with migration) stores USD rates for EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, CNY, HKD and NZD from the ECB reference rates (via Frankfurter, no API key needed). Rates are refreshed by the daily price sync, or on the first page load of the day, and fetched at most once a day. A failed fetch never breaks a sync or a page. `getFxRates(asOf?)` returns the latest rates on or before a snapshot day and falls back to the last known rate, then to built-in rates. Documented in `docs/currency.md` (#39).
- Full Scenarios & Growth view. The view now opens with a row of three cards: starting midpoint, today's valuation range (low–high, Δ, and exact low and high) and year-10 range (lowest to highest scenario, with the multiplier spread). The projection chart and Year 10 outcomes follow, then a "Valuation context & assumptions" strip that explains how projections are built (starting midpoint, year-1 shift then compounding, default raw ±10 % / sealed ±15 % spread) and what they leave out. The layout is three cards across on desktop, two plus one full-width card on tablets, and stacked on phones with a shorter chart. An empty portfolio shows only the projection empty state (#37).
- Year 10 outcomes cards beside the projection chart on the Scenarios & Growth view (stacked below it on phones): one card per scenario with its year-10 value in whole dollars, its assumptions (e.g. "Y1 −15%, then 3% annually") and a multiplier badge against the starting midpoint (e.g. 3.87×). The assumption and badge text come from the scenario inputs (`assumptionText`, `multiplierText`), so they always match the projection, and scenario colours and the `wholeUsd` formatter are now shared by the chart and the cards (#36).
- 10-year projection chart on the Scenarios & Growth view: a "Projected portfolio scenarios" panel draws Conservative (amber), Moderate (mint) and High (violet) lines from the current portfolio midpoint through the Y0/Y1/Y3/Y5/Y10 milestones, labelled "Illustrative, not a prediction". Hovering shows each scenario's value for that year in whole dollars. The chart has a text summary for screen readers, and an empty state when the portfolio has no value (#35).
- Scenario engine for the 10-year projections (`src/lib/scenarios.ts`): a pure `projectScenarios(start, inputs?, horizon?)` that projects Conservative (Y1 −15 %, then 3 %/yr), Moderate (−5 %, 8 %) and High (+10 %, 15 %) scenarios from the current portfolio midpoint. Year 1 applies the market shift, later years compound at the scenario rate. Each scenario returns its yearly values, Y0/Y1/Y3/Y5/Y10 milestones and its Y10 multiplier, and the shift, rate and horizon are configurable. Unit tests check the milestone values and multipliers against the design render; run them with the new `npm test` script (Node test runner via `tsx`) (#34).
- Hover preview of the full card art on every card row and tile (dashboard top cards, Raw Cards leaderboard, top movers, and the table, grid and by-set views). The art is the hi-res image the Pokémon TCG API sync already stores (`cards.image_large`, falling back to the small image), served resized through Next image optimisation. One shared layer in the root layout handles every card through event delegation: a card only carries a `data-card-preview` attribute from `cardPreview(card)`, which takes an `enabled` flag so previews can be switched off per card. It waits 120 ms before opening so sweeping across a table loads nothing, follows the cursor with one transform per animation frame (no React re-renders while moving), stays inside the viewport, hides on scroll, click or leaving the window, and is mouse-only (no preview on touch) (#93).
- All raw cards panel on the Raw Cards view lists every imported card, not just the top 8 and movers. It opens as a table you can sort by any column, with cost, gain, day change, filtered totals and 25 rows per page. Search and set, rarity and condition filters apply to every view. Icon buttons with hover hints switch to a card-art grid or to cards grouped by set (expandable, in card-number order, with completion). The chosen view is kept in the URL (`?view=grid`, `?view=sets`) (#89).
- Raw Cards view now shows Raw card value over time and Top movers, so the card-level history and price changes live with the other raw analytics (#32).
- Raw Cards view now also shows Set completion (owned/total cards and value per set) and Value by condition (share of raw value), completing the page alongside the header, set/rarity panels and top 8 leaderboard (#31).
- Top 8 raw card holdings leaderboard on the Raw Cards view: two numbered columns ranked by stack value, each row with thumbnail, set, rarity, variant, quantity and value (#30).
- Value by card set and Value by rarity panels on the Raw Cards view: top 5 each as horizontal bars scaled to the largest, the top item highlighted, values in compact form (e.g. $3.1k) (#29).
- Raw card intelligence header on the Raw Cards view: eyebrow, title, a line with the latest price date (ungraded only, USD) and chips for the number of sets and rarity categories in the collection (#28).
- Demo data for `npm run dev`: a separate embedded database (`.data/pglite-demo`) is filled on first start with 42 cards across 11 sets, 13 sealed products, about 14 months of price history, snapshot labels and sync runs, so every view shows how it looks when full. Real data in `.data/pglite` is never touched; set `SEED_DEV_DATA=0` to use it instead, or delete `.data/pglite-demo` to regenerate the demo. Production builds and `DATABASE_URL` are unaffected.
- GitHub pull request template and issue forms (bug report, feature request, task) in `.github/`.
- Stitch design exports in `.renders/` as the reference for the dashboard redesign.
- Rarity value-mix donut chart on the dashboard.
- `sealed_products` and `sealed_price_snapshots` tables (name, product type, set/series group, SKU, quantity, per-unit purchase price, notes; daily market/low/mid/high prices) with a Drizzle migration; existing card tables are untouched.
- Sealed product CSV import (`POST /api/import/sealed`, `docs/sealed-sample.csv`): maps name, type, set, SKU, quantity and purchase price, merges duplicate rows, adds quantity to existing products, and reports invalid rows by row number.
- Manual sealed price entry: `setSealedPrice` and a `setSealedPriceAction` server action record a current value with optional low/high per product and day (re-entering a day edits it), validated so low ≤ value ≤ high. Snapshots gain a `price_source` column (`manual`) so an API source can be added later without another migration.
- Cost basis for sealed products: `getSealed` computes per-unit cost, total cost, gain/loss, average unit cost and cost basis, and `getDashboard` now returns `sealed` and a `combined` (raw + sealed) value, cost basis and gain/loss. Holdings without a purchase price are left out of cost basis and gain, as for raw cards.
- Low / mid / high valuation: `getDashboard` returns `{ low, mid, high }` for raw cards (`rawValuation`), sealed (`sealed.valuation`, plus one per sealed holding) and the combined portfolio. Sealed uses the low/high entered with a price, otherwise a default spread (raw ±10 %, sealed ±15 %) around the market value. Method documented in `docs/valuation.md`.
- Named snapshots: a `snapshot_labels` table (with migration) holds an optional label per snapshot day, `setSnapshotLabel` / `setSnapshotLabelAction` set or clear it (empty label clears; the day must have stored prices), `listSnapshots` returns every stored day with its label and row counts, and a Snapshots panel on the dashboard lists them with an inline label field.
- Snapshot picker and historical view: a dropdown in the dashboard header selects a stored snapshot (`/?snapshot=YYYY-MM-DD`, default current). `getDashboard(asOf)` and `getSealed(asOf)` use the latest prices on or before that day, so every figure, the value chart and the header subtitle reload as of that date; an unknown day falls back to current. Holdings (quantities, cost) are not versioned, so they always reflect today.
- YTD and 30-day comparison: `getComparisons` computes the change in combined raw + sealed portfolio value from stored snapshots, as of the selected snapshot. YTD compares against the last snapshot of the previous year (or the first of this year if history starts mid-year); the 30-day baseline must be a snapshot at least 30 days old. Two cards on the dashboard show the % and $ change, or an empty state when history is too short.
- Sync status: a `sync_runs` table (with migration) records every price sync (manual button and `/api/cron/sync`) with its result, card and price counts and any error. The dashboard header shows a status chip (synced, syncing, stale after 36 hours, sync failed, never synced), the last verified sync time, "updated N mins ago", and the error when the latest attempt failed.
- Four view routes (`/`, `/sealed`, `/raw`, `/scenarios`) sharing a root-layout header (PokeVault Analytics brand and links) and footer; the sealed, raw and scenarios routes are placeholders until their epics land.
- Top app bar view tabs: the header shows the PokeVault Analytics brand and a segmented pill control (Overview, Sealed Inventory, Raw Cards, Scenarios & Growth) that routes between the four views and highlights the current one.
- Mobile bottom tab bar: under 768px the header tabs are replaced by a fixed bottom bar (Overview, Sealed, Raw Cards, Scenarios) that highlights the current view; page content gets bottom padding so the bar never covers it.
- Responsive header drawer: below 1024px the header tabs and the dashboard toolbar collapse into a hamburger drawer with a "Views & Categories" section (the four views) and a "Portfolio Controls & Tools" section (currency pill, Import CSV, Sync prices).
- Footer status bar: brand and data source (Pokémon TCG API for cards, manual prices for sealed), the selected snapshot date (or the latest price date), the current view and a page indicator (e.g. 02 / 04).
- Responsive grid per the design system: shared `page-grid` (4 columns below 768px, 8 from 768px, 12 from 1024px; gutter 0.75rem on mobile, 1.25rem above) and `page-x` (page margins 1rem / 1.5rem / 2rem) utilities, applied to the header, footer, placeholder views and the dashboard sections (KPI cards `col-span-3`, rarity mix 5 and most valuable 7 columns on desktop).
- Agent guide in `AGENTS.md` and skills in `.agents/skills/` (`start-issue`, `commit`, `changelog`) defining the changelog, branch-naming and conventional-commit rules.
- Project README with a preview of the target design, and this changelog.

### Removed
- `usd`, `wholeUsd`, `compactUsd` and `signed`; use `money`, `wholeMoney`, `compactMoney` and `signedMoney` with the page's `Money` (from `getDisplayMoney()`) (#42).

### Fixed
- Viewing a snapshot on the Overview (`/?snapshot=<day>`) no longer breaks the page: the server-rendered snapshot bar called `snapshotName()` from the client-only snapshot picker, so the page logged an error and buttons and dialogs stopped responding. The helper now lives in `src/lib/snapshot-name.ts` and is shared by both (#107).
- The Overview no longer scrolls sideways on phones: at 375px a long "Largest holding" line in the Collection structure card widened the page by about 15px, and the snapshot picker, sized to its longest option, by 3px more, so mobile browsers zoomed out. The line now truncates within the card and the picker shrinks to fit (#107).
- Compact amounts no longer show `$1000` or `$1000.0k` just below a threshold (they now read `$1.0k` and `$1.0M`), and negative compact amounts read `-$3.1k` instead of `$-3100` (#40).
- Top 8 raw card holdings leaderboard no longer makes the Raw Cards page scroll sideways on phones: below the two-column breakpoint the list now has an explicit single column, so long set · rarity · variant lines truncate instead of widening the page (#90).
- Value-over-time chart logged duplicate React key errors when every snapshot had the same total (flat history gave repeated Y-axis ticks); the axis range is now computed from the data and padded by 2 % so ticks stay distinct.

### Changed
- Inputs and segmented controls follow DESIGN.md. A `field` utility in `src/app/globals.css` gives text inputs and selects the inset fill (`#0D1733`) with a `#1E2C58` border, a faint mint border on hover, and on focus a mint border with a soft diffuse mint glow; it is used by the Raw Cards search and filters, the snapshot picker, snapshot labels and the currency search. `segmented`, `segmented-thumb` and `segment` utilities build the pill switcher: an inset container whose active segment sits on a `#1E2C58` thumb that slides between equal-width segments (no slide under reduced motion), with high-contrast text. The Raw Cards Table / Grid / By set switcher uses it in place of its mint-filled buttons. The header view tabs keep their mint pill, as in the renders (#51).
- Status chips follow the DESIGN.md data chip style through one `StatusChip` component (`src/components/status-chip.tsx`): a 10 % tint fill, a 1px border at 20 %, Space Grotesk uppercase 11px, and an optional 6px leading dot that pulses with a ping halo (off when the system asks for reduced motion). Tones are mint, sky, violet, amber, rose and neutral, or any CSS colour. The Overview sync chip pulses while the sync is live (Synced, Syncing) and stays still when Stale, failed or never synced; the Raw Cards header's set diversity and rarity count, the card condition chips (NM / LP / MP) and the Year 10 multiplier badges use it too, and the asset sheet's "Position telemetry" eyebrow uses the shared `StatusDot` (#50).
- Typography uses the DESIGN.md type scale: `src/app/globals.css` defines it as Tailwind text tokens (`text-headline-xl`, `-lg`, `-md`, `-sm` and their mobile sizes, `text-body-lg` / `-md` / `-sm`, `text-label-eyebrow` / `-md` / `-sm` and `text-metric-display`), each carrying its size, line height, tracking and weight. Page titles, card headings, metric values, eyebrows, chips, buttons and small print across every view now use them instead of one-off `text-[11px]` / `leading-[..]` / `tracking-[..]` classes, so a type change is one edit. Visible effects: mobile page titles are 28px (were 30px), 10px labels are now 11px, 11px body text is now 12px, and the Raw Cards header and donut centre use `headline-md` (24px) (#49).
- Overview no longer repeats the raw-card-only Top movers, Value by set, By rarity and By condition widgets; they moved to the Raw Cards view (Top movers, Set completion, Value by card set / rarity / condition). Value over time stays on the Overview (#32).
- Valuation context and assumptions card on the Overview: states that values are estimates, not realized proceeds, and that fees, taxes, shipping, condition, liquidity and preorder uncertainty are not accounted for.
- Collection structure panel on the Overview: sealed value split by product type (booster box, ETB, …) with percentage weights and amounts, the sealed low–high market range, and the share of sealed value held in the single largest holding; empty state until a sealed price is entered.
- Top sealed holdings panel on the Overview: the five most valuable sealed SKUs ranked as bars (top item highlighted, quantity shown) with the total SKU count, and an empty state until a sealed price is entered.
- Valuation & Concentration panel on the Overview: a low–mid–high range bar for the combined portfolio, the largest single holding (raw card or sealed stack) with its share of the portfolio, an amber concentration warning when it reaches 25 % (`CONCENTRATION_WARNING_PCT`), and a note that the range is not guaranteed realized sale proceeds.
- Portfolio value mix donut replaces the interim rarity donut: it splits combined value into Sealed and Raw cards, shows each share and amount in the legend, and highlights the matching slice (and the centre readout) when you hover a slice or legend row. The by-rarity bars below are unchanged.
- Overview summary cards: the four stat cards are now Total Portfolio (combined raw + sealed midpoint, YTD change and a sealed/raw split bar), Sealed Inventory, Raw Cards and Valuation Range (combined low – high with the midpoint). The old Total / Since last update / Gain-loss / Cards stats are folded in: total value is Total Portfolio, gain/loss and card counts move to the Sealed and Raw cards, and the day-over-day change is dropped in favour of YTD and the 30-day card.
- `start-issue` skill and `AGENTS.md` now define when to chain a branch off a blocking issue's branch and when to branch from `dev`, using GitHub "blocked by" links, which are now set on the sub-issues of every epic.
- `start-issue` skill now claims an issue first (checks board status and claim comments, sets it In Progress, releases it when done or abandoned) so only one agent works on an issue at a time.
- Restyled the dashboard to the "Executive Collectible Intelligence" design system: midnight/mint theme, Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Grotesk fonts, accent-bar summary cards and thicker progress bars.
